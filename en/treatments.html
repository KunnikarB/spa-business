🌸 Important Information

Sports massage, yoga massage and stretch massage are not typically recommended during pregnancy. These treatments may also not be suitable for people with certain medical conditions, including uncontrolled high blood pressure, diabetes, cancer, recent surgery, open wounds, bleeding disorders, use of blood-thinning medication, deep vein thrombosis (DVT) or certain spinal conditions.

If you have any medical concerns, please consult your healthcare provider before booking a massage.

🌸 What You Need to Know Before Having a Massage

  • 1 January 2026

    Nothing beats a professional massage.

    A massage can help you relax and support your overall sense of well-being. Before you arrive for your appointment, here are a few things to keep in mind.

    1. Be on time, please!

    Your massage therapist plans your appointment time specifically for you. Arriving late may reduce the amount of time available for your treatment.

    2. It is normal to undress for a massage.

    Your therapist will explain what clothing should be removed for the treatment. You will be appropriately covered with sheets or towels throughout the session.

    3. Ask for more or less pressure.

    Your therapist wants you to feel comfortable. Let them know if you would prefer more or less pressure during the massage.

    4. Tell your therapist about health concerns.

    Before your massage begins, let your therapist know about relevant health conditions, injuries, recent surgery or medication that may affect your treatment.

    5. You do not have to talk.

    The goal is to help you relax. You are welcome to enjoy your treatment quietly and communicate whenever you need something.

    6. Drink water after your appointment.

    Staying hydrated is a good general wellness practice after a massage. Take some time to rest and drink water according to your normal needs.

    7. Consider taking a warm shower first.

    A warm shower can be a relaxing way to prepare for your appointment and may help you feel comfortable before your treatment.

    8. Consider working out before your massage.

    If you exercise regularly, scheduling your massage after your workout can be a comfortable way to combine exercise with recovery and relaxation.

    9. Avoid a large meal immediately before your massage.

    A light meal a couple of hours before your appointment may help you feel more comfortable during the treatment.

    10. Communicate with your therapist.

    If you are uncomfortable, too warm, too cold or experiencing pain, speak up. Your therapist can adjust the pressure, positioning or technique where appropriate.
